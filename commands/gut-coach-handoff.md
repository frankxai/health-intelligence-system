# Gut Coach Handoff

## Purpose

Create a concise, parent-reviewed handoff for a qualified nutrition coach, dietitian, or clinician.

## Include

- Family goals and relevant practical constraints.
- Foods offered, accepted, tasted, or declined.
- What was easy or difficult in the routine.
- Questions the parent wants answered.
- Adapter identity and evidence-review date when an approved science envelope was used.
- Clear distinction between parent observations and qualified interpretations.

## Exclude

- Raw microbiome files or taxonomic tables.
- Diagnosis, causal statements, or inferred conditions.
- Unreviewed free text.
- Data outside the parent's chosen scope.

## Review Gate

The parent must review and approve the export. A qualified professional owns any clinical interpretation, medical nutrition therapy, and care decision.

Use `templates/gut-coach-handoff.md`.
