# Gut First-Week Plan

## Purpose

Turn a validated family context and approved science envelope into a conservative seven-day meal-planning experiment.

## Rules

- Use only ordinary foods allowed by the allergy and exclusion gates.
- Anchor every meal in at least one accepted food.
- Add no more than one optional learning food at a time.
- Always offer a familiar fallback.
- Stay within the preparation-time and budget constraints.
- Do not promise gut, symptom, growth, or disease outcomes.
- Do not prescribe supplements, doses, fasting, elimination diets, or medical nutrition therapy.

## Workflow

1. Re-run consent, age, allergy, scope, science-adapter, and claims gates.
2. Build a small candidate set from accepted foods plus approved food categories.
3. Remove allergens, avoid foods, clinician exclusions, and unsupported categories deterministically.
4. Draft seven meals using `templates/gut-first-week-plan.md`.
5. Add school, travel, batch-prep, and picky-eater adaptations only where relevant.
6. Create an evidence receipt listing inputs used, inputs excluded, gate results, authority, and review status.
7. Ask the parent to approve, edit, or reject the plan.

## Termination

Block plan generation when exclusions leave no safe candidate set or when the request crosses into medical care. Produce a qualified-professional question list instead.
