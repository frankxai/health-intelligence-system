# Gut Weekly Review

## Purpose

Summarize what the family tried, what was practical, and what to discuss next without interpreting symptoms or claiming biological change.

## Inputs

- Meals offered, accepted, tasted, or skipped.
- Parent-observed practicality: time, cost, school fit, leftovers, and stress.
- Optional neutral notes written by the parent.

## Workflow

1. Exclude identifiers and unnecessary medical detail.
2. Summarize adherence and practicality as observations, not causes.
3. Separate `worked`, `hard`, `not tried`, and `question for coach`.
4. Suggest at most one next low-pressure food-learning experiment.
5. If symptoms, weight, growth, abnormal results, or treatment questions appear, stop the ordinary-wellness loop and route to a qualified professional.
6. Produce an evidence receipt and ask the parent to approve the summary.

## Output

- Parent-reviewed weekly reflection.
- One optional next experiment.
- Coach or clinician questions.
- Explicit exclusions and route decisions.
