# Performance learning ledger

Checked 6 September 2026. Generic public reference; no participant records or individualized plan.

A compelling performance presentation can contain motivation, autobiography, scientific claims and incomplete transcription in the same paragraph. Preserve those distinctions before turning it into a product rule.

## Claim handling

| Topic | Evidence-grounded handling | Source |

| Full IRONMAN distance | 3.8 km swim, 180 km bike and 42.2 km run; do not reuse an incomplete distance note | [Official athlete guide](https://www.ironman.com/sites/default/files/2026-06/IMFR2026_guide_athlete_EN.pdf) |
| Heart attacks in Germany | More than 300,000 annually corresponds to roughly 822/day. An approximate event estimate is not a daily death count or a 2026 measured total | [German Heart Foundation](https://herzstiftung.de/ihre-herzgesundheit/gesund-bleiben/herzgesundheit-im-alltag) |
| Maximum heart-rate estimate | 220 minus age is a rough population guide; it does not measure individual maximum or set personal safe zones | [AHA](https://www.heart.org/en/healthy-living/exercise-and-physical-activity/fitness-basics/target-heart-rates) |
| A separate universal women's formula | Do not adopt 226 minus age as a universal standard; population equations differ and remain estimates | [Gulati et al. (2010)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20585008/) |
| Very low resting pulse | An athlete's reported figure is not a target or a score of overall health | [AHA heart-rate context](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/high-blood-pressure/the-facts-about-high-blood-pressure/all-about-heart-rate-pulse) |
| Percentage training zones | Preserve the denominator and method. Heart-rate maximum, reserve and oxygen uptake are different quantities; fat-oxidation intensity varies among individuals | [Achten and Jeukendrup (2003)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14598198/) |
| Four-minute intervals | NTNU's common 4×4 description uses approximately 85–95% maximum heart rate during work, three-minute active recovery, warm-up and cool-down. It is not a four-minute all-out prescription | [NTNU CERG](https://www.ntnu.edu/cerg/advice) |
| Athlete protein range | DGE describes roughly 1.2–2.0 g/kg depending on context. The upper end is not everyone's minimum | [DGE position paper](https://www.germanjournalsportsmedicine.com/archive/archive-2020/issue-7-8-9/protein-intake-in-sports-position-of-the-working-group-sports-nutrition-of-the-german-nutrition-society-dge/) |
| “Carbohydrates make people fat” | This blanket causal rule is not justified; controlled diet comparisons show a more complex picture and do not establish one ideal diet for everyone | [NIH controlled feeding study](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-study-compares-low-fat-plant-based-diet-low-carb-animal-based-diet) |
| Endurance fueling | Sports carbohydrate guidance depends on activity demands; do not automatically combine increased endurance training with restrictive eating or fasting | [DGE carbohydrates in sport](https://www.dge.de/wissenschaft/stellungnahmen-und-positionspapiere/kohlenhydrate-in-der-sporternaehrung/) |
| Zero-sugar cola, magnesium and cramps | Research on caffeine and urinary mineral excretion does not establish that a particular beverage caused deficiency or an individual's cramps | [Bergman et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2402180/) |
| Short naps | A short-nap experiment supports bounded alertness findings after restricted sleep; a timer is not a sleep-stage measurement | [Brooks and Lack (2006)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16796222/) |

| Precise conscious/unconscious percentages | No operational definition or supporting source was supplied for a 5%/95% split; do not encode it as a scientific fact | Source-needed claim |
| Knowing versus doing | COM-B can organize capability, opportunity and motivation questions; it does not diagnose a hidden belief or validate this product | [Michie et al. (2011)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1748-5908-6-42) |

These summaries are educational source corrections. They do not select training frequency, calories, diet, supplements, fasting or treatment for a person.

## Arithmetic and units

Synthetic example: 80 kg × 2 g/kg = 160 g protein. Using 4 kcal/g gives 640 kcal, or 25.6% of an illustrative 2,500 kcal total. Those assumptions are not assessed needs or an endorsed target.

Retain the actual denominator and unit. An unexplained “55” remains unresolved; it must not become a VO2 value, age, percentage or nutrition target. A percentage is incomplete without what it is a percentage of.

## Storytelling and attribution

Autobiographical race counts, unusually low pulse values, annual training days, large KPI inventories and remarkable older-adult exercise examples should remain attributed reports unless independently supported.

An ambiguous opening story can illustrate the audience adding missing information. Preserve the actual story and uncertainty; do not invent a transcript or claim the same experience as your own.

Five factors such as identity, vision, emotional focus, strategy and practice may be useful teaching structure. Identify the speaker's framework and distinguish it from an original operational synthesis. Do not convert a rhetorical framework into a validated model of life outcomes.

A quote about realizing one has only one life should not acquire an unverified historical author during republication.

## Product rules

- Capture observation, claim, interpretation, question and proposed experiment separately.
- A missed action does not establish unconscious resistance.
- Ask about practical capability and opportunity alongside the user's own motivation account.
- Missing information remains unknown; a pause or closed goal is legitimate.
- Track a small set of observations that change decisions.
- Keep personal records and private raw notes in the private instance.
- Route personal clinical decisions to qualified care; do not infer urgency or diagnosis from this ledger.
