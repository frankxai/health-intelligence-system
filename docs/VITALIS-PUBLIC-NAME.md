# Vitalis and Velora — Public Name Hold

**Screened:** 2026-08-24
**Status:** both naked names are held from public health or fitness product use. This is
a collision screen, not legal clearance or legal advice.

## Durable decision

- **Health Intelligence System** remains the descriptive public health protocol,
  evidence, privacy, and clinician-preparation source.
- **Agentic Life OS** remains the descriptive public multi-domain runtime.
- **Health Stewardship Guide** is the neutral public health agent label.
- **Movement and Training Foundations Educator** and **Training Intelligence Pack** are
  neutral public training labels.
- **Vitalis** may remain emotionally meaningful as a private memorial health-steward
  codename inside `frankx-life-os`, together with the public tribute “Built in memory of
  Witali.” It is not a public app, package, service, marketplace agent, or product name.
- **Velora** remains reserved for Arcanea's established courage/action Executor persona.
  It is not a public fitness, gym, recovery, or health name.

Machine-facing IDs remain neutral and stable:
`agent_bios_steward` and `agent_training_foundations_educator`.

## Why the old candidate decision was reopened

The screen found exact-name AI health, biomarker/lab-analysis, personal Health OS,
health-companion, and care products using Vitalis. It also found an exact Velora
training/food/recovery/AI-coach product and a Velora Health Companion, while Arcanea
already implements Velora as its Executor. “Candidate pending clearance” would leave too
much room for an agent to publish a conflicting identity.

## Release gate

Do not use either held name in public product metadata, app-store copy, domains, handles,
packages, marketplace agents, or commercial health claims. A future exception requires
a fresh exact/phonetic/related-goods search and qualified human review across relevant
jurisdictions; adding a compound does not automatically remove confusion risk.

Do not create empty `vitalis-*` or `velora-*` repositories, purchase domains, change DNS,
file marks, or revive the earlier candidate ADR from this document.

## Product topology

Keep [`health-intelligence-system`](../README.md) as the public health protocol and use
the public `agentic-life-os` runtime to compose it. Real personal data belongs in a
separate encrypted runtime governed by the private `frankx-life-os` configuration, never
in either public repository.

The machine-readable bounded domain registry is
[`bios/registry/public-domain-agents.json`](../bios/registry/public-domain-agents.json).
