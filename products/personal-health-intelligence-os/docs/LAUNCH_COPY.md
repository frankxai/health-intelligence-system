## Founder journal / product introduction

Health notes are easy to collect and surprisingly difficult to use a month later.

A sleep estimate ends up in a chat. A product label stays in a photo. A lab report sits in Drive. The next review starts with reconstructing what happened.

We are building Personal Health Intelligence OS around a simple requirement: the record should remain useful when you change your assistant.

The first release candidate is an editable Google or Notion workspace. It gives daily capture a consistent shape, keeps sources alongside records, distinguishes unknown values from zeros, and makes weekly review repeatable. All public examples are synthetic.

The assistant layer follows the account's actual capabilities. A connection has to retrieve the right record, propose a precise change, write only with authorization and read the result back. A manual export is a supported path too.

This product organizes your own observations and documents. It does not prescribe supplements, interpret clinical tests or promise an optimized body.

The next test is practical: can a new customer install it, make a useful check-in and export a record without founder intervention? That is the release gate we are working toward.

## Pilot email draft

Subject: Personal Health Intelligence OS: a private workspace pilot

We are testing an editable health-record workspace for people already using Google or Notion.

It brings daily observations, source documents and weekly reviews into one consistent structure. You keep your workspace and choose whether to connect an assistant. All setup demonstrations use synthetic records; you enter real information privately.

This is a template pilot, not a treatment or supplement-advice service. Automatic Google–Notion synchronization is not included. The test is whether you can install the selected edition, complete a useful check-in and export a record without founder intervention.

The Google and Notion launch-price hypotheses are €29 and €39. Final price, included support, compatibility and delivery/refund terms must be confirmed before a paid pilot opens.

The next release update will include the verified installation route and exact scope.
