# Your health records. In your workspace.

A daily check-in, a source-linked health library, and a weekly review you can keep using with the tools you already own.

Personal Health Intelligence OS brings your observations, product labels, food records, sleep notes, training logs, documents and decisions into an editable workspace. Choose Google Workspace or Notion. Keep your accounts, credentials and AI provider relationship.

**Release candidate 0.1.0.** This page is a publication draft. Purchase buttons remain disabled until delivery and independent customer installation pass. Prices below are proposed launch prices.

## Start with today

Record what you know: your sleep, energy, fluids, movement and a brief note. Leave unknown measurements blank. The workspace keeps the difference between a reported value, an estimate, a hypothesis and a cited source.

The first useful check-in has a ten-minute installation target. That is an acceptance target; it has not yet been demonstrated across independent customer accounts.

## Use your preferred assistant

The instruction pack covers data stewardship, label reconciliation, food records, daily capture, experiment review, evidence research and weekly summaries. Use it with manual exports or a supported connection that passes the account verification test.

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini expose different tools in different accounts. Read/write access, approvals, plans and automation support are verified for your selected setup. The product does not include deployed autonomous workers or background sync.

## Built for ownership

No vendor health-data account is required. The commercial demo uses synthetic records. Your purchased copy belongs in your private workspace; public sharing and assistant writes are optional choices. Export instructions preserve stable record IDs, units, timestamps and provenance.

This is a recordkeeping and reflection product. It does not diagnose, interpret clinical tests, prescribe doses, recommend treatment or replace an existing care plan. Health records are sensitive; choose account access deliberately.
