# Buyer FAQ

Personal Health Intelligence OS · 0.1.0 publication draft

## What am I purchasing?

An editable workspace template package, synthetic examples, installation materials and portable assistant instruction documents. Google and Notion are separate editions. Final included files and compatibility are listed in the accepted release manifest. The seller must bind a working delivery route before taking orders.

## Is this a health app subscription?

Stage 1 is a one-time template product. Your workspace and assistant subscriptions are separate. Hosted software, automatic sync and ongoing managed health services are not included.

## Does it tell me which supplements or treatments to use?

No. It organizes labels and user-reported intake, identifies duplicate/missing data and supports descriptive reviews. It does not prescribe doses, change medication, interpret clinical findings or recommend treatment/training therapy. A product amount is a source record, not an instruction to take it.

## Can I use it without an AI connection?

Yes. Enter records and use the workspace manually. The optional instruction pack also works with selected exports. No connection is required for the first check-in.

## Does every assistant support automatic writes?

No. Tools vary by platform, plan, account, client, scopes and workspace policy. A synthetic read/propose/write/readback test establishes what your selected connection can do. Manual operation is the fallback. No unattended cross-platform workflow is shipped in Stage 1.

## Is all my data synchronized between Google and Notion?

No. Each standalone edition owns its own records. The Complete Connected design assigns a single canonical home per entity and read-only mirrors. The automatic adapter belongs to Stage 2 and must pass conflict, audit, export and revocation tests before it is advertised as available.

## Where do my records live?

In the customer-owned workspace and file locations you choose. The template vendor needs no health-data access. When you connect an assistant, selected data is processed under your relationship with that provider and the scopes you approve. Workspace storage does not imply zero retention or EU-only AI processing.

## Can a partner set it up without seeing my health history?

That is the intended setup model. A qualified partner works with synthetic data while you perform sign-ins. You enter real information privately after handoff. Temporary access is removed and tested before acceptance. Partner Setup is a separate service; no partner capacity has been booked through this draft.

## Can I import wearable or clinical data?

Keep original exports and source documents. Supported imports must match the documented release schema and validation. Automatic Apple Health, device APIs, hospital portals and certified FHIR exchange are not bundled. Unverified source values remain unverified after import.

## Can I export or delete my records?

Yes, using the documented workspace export/deletion workflow. CSV alone does not preserve files, formulas, access rights or Notion relations; export the canonical JSON/relation manifest and attachments as well. Trash, provider chats and backups have independent retention; disconnecting an assistant does not erase earlier chat copies.

## Will product upgrades overwrite my records?

They must not. Back up first, apply documented additive migrations to a copy and check record counts/IDs/relations. A supported upgrade preserves customer data and customization. Unsupported schema changes require explicit mapping and review.

## Are the examples real customer data?

No. Commercial examples are synthetic. Personal source material and identifying records do not belong in templates, screenshots or launch content.

## Can I resell the source?

The existing open-source core is MIT licensed and its rights and notices remain intact. Paid value covers curation, packaging, maintenance and optional services. Any separately licensed original assets must be identified clearly; no contradictory exclusivity or blanket resale restriction is imposed on MIT-derived material.

## What support, refunds and updates are included?

The seller must specify the actual support contact, response scope, maintenance period and refund/consumer-rights terms at checkout before publication. No lifetime updates, medical support, guaranteed outcome or blanket no-refund condition is promised by this release candidate.
