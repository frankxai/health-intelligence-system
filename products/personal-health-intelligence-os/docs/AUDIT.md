# Source recovery and reuse decision

The build inspected the named private Drive folder, master protocol and six structured tracker tab headers, the 90-day Notion protocol, existing Health OS, seven core legacy template schemas, the template quickstart and SOP library, the Health/Longevity workspace entry and HIS concept record. It also inspected both existing health repositories, their README and package metadata, the current repo instructions, and the existing Health Second Brain product/analyzer implementation.

This is a bounded asset audit, not an exhaustive reconstruction of historical conversations, every family branch or every personal health record. Personal protocols were read for architectural decisions; measurements, health conditions, routines, products and private source links were not copied into commercial data or examples. The private tracker supplied structural headers. No private daily measurement rows were present in the bounded read.

Preserved decisions: one fact once, source-linked observations, fast daily capture, deeper symptom episodes, weekly review, explicit hypotheses, separate evidence strength and applicability, individual control over AI access, and one chosen system of record per entity. The general 90-day experimental cadence became an optional bounded-experiment method. Individual doses and condition-specific recommendations were excluded from the commercial default.

The legacy shareable Notion master contains additional family and research child branches. It was not duplicated wholesale for commercial delivery. A new synthetic-only derivative reuses its useful schema and capture patterns without retaining personal or unreviewed child links.

The implementation extends frankxai/health-intelligence-system under products/personal-health-intelligence-os rather than opening a repository. The newer Health Second Brain development source provides the ownership and descriptive-analysis boundary. The older Life-Commons copy was inspected for duplication risk and left unchanged. Existing FrankX Stripe webhook/dependency evidence informs commerce planning; no live account credentials or products were inspected or activated.

Private source identifiers remain outside the commercial pack. Customer master URLs in installation-manifest.json refer only to the newly created synthetic release assets.
