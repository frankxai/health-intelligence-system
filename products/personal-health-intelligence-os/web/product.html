Questions, answered

Before you make it yours.

Is this another health subscription?

The proposed Stage 1 product is a one-time workspace template and instruction pack. Your existing workspace or AI provider may charge separately. No recurring subscription is required by this release candidate.

Will every AI assistant edit my records?

No universal write capability is promised. Capabilities vary by account, platform, region, connector and permissions. A supported setup can use a read-only summary or manual copy path when writes are unavailable. Any change should produce a clear receipt.

Can I use it without connecting an AI?

Yes. Daily entry, libraries and reviews are native workspace workflows. AI assistance is optional.

Can I import my existing records?

Optional import starts with a small copy and a validated mapping. Preserve the source, keep provenance, and reconcile duplicates before accepting records. Custom wearable or clinical imports are outside the fixed setup scope.

Does the Complete Edition sync automatically today?

No. Its source-of-truth, conflict, identifier and export architecture is a Stage 2 design. Automated synchronization is not shipped in this Stage 1 release candidate.

How do updates preserve my data?

Keep customer records in a separate customer copy. An upgrade adds or migrates structure after backup and mapping review; it never replaces a customer workspace with a new demo. The release package documents the version and compatibility boundary.

What happens to my data when I stop?

Your records remain in your workspace. Export them using the platform’s documented tools, revoke integrations, and follow the deletion checklist for copies, exports and trash retention. We do not promise that deleting one workspace instantly deletes every provider-held backup.
